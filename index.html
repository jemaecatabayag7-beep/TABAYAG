About Boracay

A tropical paradise in the Philippines

Boracay is a small island in the Western Visayas region of the Philippines. It is famous for its beautiful beaches, clear blue waters, stunning sunsets, and exciting activities. The island is a popular destination for both local and international travelers.

Beautiful Spots

Explore some of Boracay's most popular destinations

White Beach

Known for its soft white sand, clear waters, and spectacular sunsets, White Beach is Boracay's most famous attraction.

Puka Beach

A peaceful beach known for its natural beauty, relaxing atmosphere, and unique crushed-shell sand.

Willy's Rock

A famous rock formation near White Beach that provides a beautiful backdrop for photographs and sightseeing.

Travel Guide

Things to know before visiting Boracay

Getting There

Travelers can fly to nearby airports and continue their journey to Boracay by land and boat.

Best Time to Visit

The dry season is generally popular for beach activities, outdoor adventures, and sightseeing.

Where to Stay

Boracay offers a variety of accommodations, from affordable stays to beachfront resorts.

Travel Tips

Keep the island clean, respect local rules, and help protect Boracay's natural environment.
